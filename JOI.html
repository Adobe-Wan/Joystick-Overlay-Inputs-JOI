How To

Bind or Rebind a Widget

Select the widget and press Bind or Rebind under its name, then move the stick or pedal, or press the button, when asked. Bind Inputs on the Setup panel walks through every widget that isn't bound yet.

More in the README: A device isn't showing up

Arrange and Align

Drag a widget to move it: smart guides snap it to the screen center, the edges and other widgets, and Alt drags without snapping. Drag a box or Shift+click to select several, then use the align icons at the top of the panel.

More in the README: Positioning

Back Up and Share a Profile

In Profiles & Share, press Download profile.js and save it in the folder that holds JOI.html, so your layouts survive OBS clearing its cache. To share one profile, send its Download .json file; the other pilot adds it with Import a Profile.

More in the README: Profiles and presets

Add to OBS and Capture the Game View

In OBS, add a Browser source, check Local file, pick JOI.html and set it to your canvas size. Right-click the source and choose Interact to edit. Capture Game View on the Setup panel puts a screenshot of your game behind the editor, so you can place widgets around the HUD.

More in the README: Set up in OBS

Overlay Lags While You Fly

If the widgets keep up with OBS in front but fall behind in the game, the game is likely using all of your GPU, and OBS's browser waits its turn. Try these in order: cap the game's frame rate a little below what your GPU reaches; in OBS Settings, Advanced, turn off Browser Source Hardware Acceleration and restart OBS, so the overlay draws on the CPU, out of the game's way; and set Projector Motion to Static. Settings, Performance, Smoothness Check shows whether it helped.

Edit Widgets Together

Drag a box around several widgets, or Shift+click them, and the side panel edits all of them at once.

Use it to match sizes, line them up in rows and columns, or give the whole set one color theme and Cockpit Look.

    Capture From OBS

    JOI asks OBS for a screenshot of your game capture and puts it behind the editor. The game has to be on screen, so JOI counts down while you switch to it. In OBS, open Tools, WebSocket Server Settings, turn on Enable WebSocket server, then use Show Connect Info to copy the port and password here.

    Off: you type it once each time JOI loads. The password lets anything that has it control OBS, and other local pages on this PC can read what JOI remembers. It is never in Profiles & Share files.
